# Minutes and rate dependence

These numbers describe the pre-holdout out-of-sample PIT pairs. They are not a calibration slice of the points PMF.

`u_m` is the randomized PIT of realized minutes. `u_r` is the randomized PIT of `min(pts / minutes, 6)`. Both use the sampler CDFs, tails included. The tier is that row's predicted minutes q50.

Under independence, Spearman correlation is 0. Inside each `u_m` decile, mean `u_r` is 0.500 and the SD of `u_r` is 0.289. `P(u_m < 0.1, u_r < 0.1) / 0.01` and `P(u_m < 0.1, u_r > 0.9) / 0.01` both equal 1.0. The share of either uniform below 0.05 or above 0.95 is 5%.

Deciles of `u_m` are `[0.0, 0.1), …, [0.9, 1.0]`.
