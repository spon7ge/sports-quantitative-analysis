How each test should look like:
Step 1 — Collect data
Historical odds, closing odds, game outcomes, team strength, rest days, injuries where reliably recorded, and other features available before the game.

Step 2 — Build a baseline
Convert odds into implied probabilities, remove the bookmaker's margin, and compare those probabilities with a simple Elo or logistic regression model.

Step 3 — Test one hypothesis
For example, investigate whether rest disadvantages add predictive information after accounting for team strength and the market's existing expectations.

Step 4 — Evaluate honestly
Use chronological holdouts, log loss, Brier score, calibration, and realistic odds available at the time. Keep feature selection separate from the final test period.

Step 5 — Write up the result
Document the hypothesis, methodology, baselines, uncertainty, failed experiments, and whether the evidence supports your original idea.
